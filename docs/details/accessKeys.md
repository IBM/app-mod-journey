# Access Keys

- Access Keys control which features and capabilities are available to a user, from initial evaluation through to full-scale enterprise deployment
- Available in Application Modernization Accelerator version 5.0.0 and later
- There are three types: **Trial**, **Proof of Concept (PoC)**, and **Full**

## Trial Access

- Automatically applied on install, no setup required
- Valid for 90 days
- Full capability with sample data; with your own data you can scan applications and view recommendations and issues
- No detailed modernization help, no artefact downloads, no estate-wide reporting
- Best for initial evaluation

## Proof of Concept (PoC) Access

- Obtained from your IBM representative using your instance UUID
- Unlocks full capability for a limited number of applications (usually 3)
- Includes detailed modernization help, artefact downloads, and automation for those applications
- Estate-wide reporting remains unavailable

## Full Access

- Downloaded from IBM Passport Advantage once you have product entitlement
- Unrestricted capability across your entire application estate, including estate-wide reporting and guidance

## Applying a Key

- Click the Access Key information in the product header to open the Access Key panel
- Click **Upload access key** and select your `.key` file
- The key is validated and applied immediately; the page refreshes with your new capabilities

For further information, see [Access Keys](https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/ama?topic=entitlement-access-keys)
